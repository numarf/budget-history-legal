# Privacy Policy — Budget History

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Budget History is designed so that **we never see your financial data**.

**What stays on your phone.** All accounts, transactions, budgets and settings are stored only on your device. Bank SMS messages (Android, if you turn SMS import on) are read and parsed on the device and are never uploaded. The optional AI coach runs entirely on your phone.

**Backups and sync.** If you turn on Backup & sync, your data is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM before it is uploaded to *your own* Google Drive (hidden app folder) or iCloud account. The encryption key is protected by your backup password and a recovery key that only you hold. We cannot read, recover or reset your backups.

**Network requests.** The app downloads exchange rates (open.er-api.com) and, only if you choose to, the AI model file (huggingface.co). These requests contain no personal data.

**No tracking.** No analytics, advertising identifiers or third-party trackers are included.

**Your control.** Delete individual records in the app, delete the AI model in Settings, remove the backup folder from your cloud account, or uninstall the app to remove everything from the device.
